Features

Everything your street needs

One app to find food stalls, skilled workers, and everything in between — wherever you are in Mzansi.

📍
Live map pins
See vendors and workers pinned in real time on a full map view. Filter by type, category, or payment method in one tap.
🔵
Fixed & roaming locations
Vendors can mark a permanent spot. Workers like plumbers or cleaners can set a roaming zone — a shaded area showing where they operate.
📸
Stall photos
Vendors upload photos of their stall so you can spot them easily. Reviewers can add photos too — making every pin richer over time.
⭐
Community reviews
Rate and review any pin. Leave comments so your neighbours know what to expect before they walk over.
👤
Claim your pin
Vendors and workers can claim community-added pins and take ownership — update details, add photos, and respond to reviews.
🌍
South African languages
Built with support for isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho, Setswana and more — the app speaks your language.
How it works

Simple for everyone

1
Open the map
Launch PinIt and see all vendors and workers near your current location instantly.
2
Tap a pin
See photos, categories, payment methods, operating hours, and community reviews.
3
Add or claim a pin
Spotted a vendor not on the map? Add them. If you are the vendor, claim your pin and keep it up to date.
4
Leave a review
Share your experience so the next person knows exactly what to expect.
Pin types

Two kinds of pins, one map

PinIt serves two distinct communities — vendors selling goods and workers offering skills — all discoverable in the same place.

🛒
Street vendors
Food stalls, clothing, hardware, produce — anything sold from a fixed spot or regular location on the street.
Food Clothing Produce Hardware Airtime Crafts
🔧
Informal workers
Plumbers, electricians, cleaners, gardeners — skilled people looking for work, visible in the areas they operate.
